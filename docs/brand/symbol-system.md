# Symbol System: Living Mark Sprint

## Goal
Define a symbol that feels alive without requiring on-page movement: it should imply motion through shape tension, orbit flow, and asymmetry.

## Concept A: Tidal Moon
- **Idea:** Crescent moon cut by a thin orbital arc.
- **Why it works:** Calm, reflective, and spatial.
- **Risk:** Can read as generic if the cut/arc geometry is too soft.

## Concept B: Pulse Infinity
- **Idea:** Infinity loop with one side compressed and one side expanded.
- **Why it works:** Conveys continuity, learning, and energy transfer.
- **Risk:** Can feel abstract without a distinct signature angle.

## Concept C: Event Horizon Ring
- **Idea:** Offset ring around a dense core with one bright escape arc.
- **Why it works:** Space-inspired, high-energy, and naturally “alive.”
- **Risk:** Needs careful stroke thickness to stay legible at favicon size.

## Final Selection
**Selected:** Concept C (Event Horizon Ring)

## Rationale
- Strongest fit for your tech-futuristic direction.
- Feels dynamic through implied rotation and gravitational pull.
- Scales well from favicon to hero accent with simple geometry.

## Usage Rules
- Keep outer ring and core centered; only the accent arc is offset.
- Use a dark base with electric accents (cyan/violet).
- Maintain minimum clear space equal to core radius.
- Avoid gradients below 16px; use flat fill at tiny sizes.

## Do / Don't
- **Do:** place the mark on clean surfaces with high contrast.
- **Do:** pair with simple typography (`BAMP`) in navigation/header use.
- **Don't:** stretch or skew the mark.
- **Don't:** animate with bounce; if animated later, use slow orbit/fade only.
